## A review finding ruled not a defect (planning agent)

A terminus review of the reply box batch blocked on this: output that arrives late from an earlier Codex turn, while a newer turn is in flight, is counted as unread when the newer turn ends and not at once. It was carded as `late-codex-output-during-a-newer-turn`, and the card was removed the same day at Michael's suggestion, since there is nothing to build.

- **Why it is not a defect.** Attention is one value, decided in order: a pending request, an unsupervised runtime, a turn in flight, an error, unread output, idle. A conversation with a turn in flight reads as running whatever it has unread, so it could not show as unread during that turn either way. Counting the late output at once would move a counter and nothing on the page. When the newer turn ends it is unread with the rest.
- **Nothing is hidden meanwhile.** A failed background job's result is in the history as a failed action the moment it arrives.
- **Where the ruling lives.** The canon's `bookkeeping-never-creates-attention` gained a line in its list of what not to flag, so a review does not block on it again.
- **If this is ever read differently,** the review's fix was to handle output marked as after its turn before the branch for an active turn, in the unread accounting in `internal/service/live.go`.

## A model switch left a Claude conversation spinning (pull request 3, `plorenz`)
